Privacy Policy

Soshule, by Pranjul Dhiman. Effective 21 November 2026, updated 24 November 2026.

Soshule helps you plan Instagram Reels and X posts, reminds you when it's time to post, and coaches you as you grow. This policy explains what information Soshule handles, why, who else is involved, and the choices you have. We've tried to write it in plain language.

The short version

  • We collect what Soshule needs to work: your Google sign-in details, your setup answers and the posts you schedule.
  • Your videos never leave your phone. Soshule doesn't upload them.
  • Soshule never posts for you and never asks for your Instagram or X password.
  • When you use the coach or the writing help, your message goes to an AI provider to write the answer. We don't keep coach conversations.
  • No ads, no selling your data, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, any time.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in

You sign in with your Google account. Google shares your name, email address and an account identifier with us, and we create a Soshule account linked to them. We never see your Google password.

When you set up your plan

  • The name you'd like to be called
  • Where you post (Instagram, X or both), what you post about, your goal and how much time you have each week
  • A writing sample, if you choose to give one, and the three words that describe your writing voice
  • Your phone's time zone, so reminders arrive at the right local time
  • How far you got through setup, so you can pick up where you left off

When you schedule a post

  • The caption, or each post of an X thread
  • When it's scheduled, in your time zone
  • Its status (scheduled, done, missed or deleted) and whether it has a video. The video itself stays on your phone.

When you use the coach or writing help

The coach, "Write hooks for me", "Make it punchier" and "Learn my voice" send what you typed to an AI provider (see section 3), along with the context needed to answer well: your name, niche, goal, time per week, writing-voice words and open plan tasks. We count how many of these requests you make each day, so everyone gets a fair daily limit. We don't store your coach conversations on our servers.

2. What stays on your phone

Some information is kept only on your device and never sent to us:

  • The videos you pick for Reels, and their preview images. Soshule uses Android's photo picker, so it only sees the clips you choose.
  • Your reminders, which your phone schedules itself.
  • Your sign-in session, kept in your phone's encrypted storage.
  • A copy of your posts and setup, so the app works without a signal.

When you post

When you tap "Post it now", Soshule opens Instagram or X on your phone with your video or text, and copies your caption so you can paste it. What you then post is handled by Instagram or X under their own privacy policies. Soshule isn't affiliated with Instagram, Meta, X or X Corp.

When the law requires it

We may disclose information if we're legally required to, for example by a court order, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app: build your plan, save your posts, sync them between your phone and your account, and remind you at the right time.
  • To give coaching and writing help that fits your niche and voice.
  • To keep the service fair and working: daily limits on AI requests, and fixing errors.

We don't use your information to train AI models, and we don't build advertising profiles.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, to remind you when it's time to post. Soshule works without them; you just won't get reminders.
  • Photos and videos, through Android's photo picker, limited to the clips you choose.

Soshule doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or full storage access.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while you have a Soshule account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Today → Settings → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, setup answers, posts and request counts from our database, and the copy on your phone. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups expire within 7 days.

If you can't open the app, you can ask us to delete your account.

7. Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database lets each account read and change only its own information. Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's encrypted storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your setup answers in the app (Settings → Redo setup), and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to:

  • get a copy of the information we hold about you,
  • correct something that's wrong,
  • delete your account and information, or
  • raise a concern or complaint about how we handle your information.

We'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example in India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act or in the EU or UK under the GDPR, you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

9. Children

Soshule is meant for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, for example a new kind of information or a new provider, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
