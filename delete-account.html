Delete your Soshule account

Soshule, by Pranjul Dhiman

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Soshule.
  2. On Today, tap your initial in the top corner to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email pranjulflows@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Soshule account". We'll delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done. We may ask you to confirm the request, to make sure it really comes from you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and account identifier
  • Your setup answers: niche, goal, time per week, writing sample and writing-voice words, time zone
  • Every post you scheduled, and its schedule
  • Your daily count of coach requests

Videos, reminders and the app's offline copy live only on your phone. Deleting your account in the app removes them; uninstalling the app also does.

What may be kept, and for how long

Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups expire within 7 days. We keep nothing else.

Anything you've already posted on Instagram or X stays there. Delete it in those apps if you want it gone.

More about how Soshule handles your information is in our privacy policy.
